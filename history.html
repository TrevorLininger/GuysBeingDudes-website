History

2017

The Inception

🏆 Champion: Zach Vredenburg

In the fall of 2017, in Baldwin City, Kansas, a group of 10 courageous individuals came together to form a fantasy football league. Our founding members: Trevor Lininger, Tyler Cawley, Zach Vredenburg, Gage Kiesling, Wes Summers, Lake Johnson, Jim Joyner, and Nick Yarbrough. This is where our story begins. Despite a dominant performance from Commissioner Gage Kiesling in the regular season, it was Zach Vredenburg who cemented his legacy as the first champion of the GBD Fantasy Football League. This inaugural season would eventually become one of the greatest fantasy football leagues known to man.

2018

The Sequel

🏆 Champion: Tyler Cawley

The league returned for its second season, where innagural champion Zach Vredenburg seemed determined to defend his title. He was upset in the championship game by Tyler Cawley in a rematch from 2017's Championship Game.

2019

A Season of Change

🏆 Champion: Nick Yarbrough

After dominating the first two seasons in the league, Zach Vredenburg and Tyler Cawley left the league...
Baker Baseball alums Ethan Jacks and Austin Chisam answered the noble call to join the league, allowing the lague to heal its newly created fractures. A competitive season yielded a new champion, as Nick Yarbrough defeated Wes Summers in the championship.

2020

The "Bubble" Season

🏆 Champion: Jim Joyner

With the world on lockdown due to the COVID-19 pandemic, all eyes looked to the GBD Fantasy Football League for an escape from the chaos. As best they could, the league soldiered on through a tightly contested regular season. First place in the regular season was Jim Joyner with an 8-5 record. The rest of the league was tightly pakced, with 5 teams finishing with a 7-6 record. Jim cememted his legacy as 2020's best team, defeating Trevor Lininger in the championship game.

2021

Commissioner's Triumph

🏆 Champion: Gage Kiesling

As the 2021 season approached, the league was once again gutted by the departure of a league member. Ethan Jacks left the league after only 2 seasons.
In his place, a young up and comer bravely stepped up to the plate. Kip Unruh was selected as the 10th member of the league.
The real story of this season came in the playoffs, as regular season champion Wes Summers, who finished with a 13-1 record was defeated by Ryker Johnson in the semi-finals, setting up what would come to be known as one of the premier rivalries in the league, as he would face off against the Commissioner himself, Gage Kiesling. Gage would ultimately come out victorious.

2022

Back to Back

🏆 Champion: Gage Kiesling

Determined to remove the commissioner from his throne, the league was set ablaze with a renewed intensity. Jim took the regular season crown with a record of 10-4 and looked primed to return to glory. That is until he ran into Gage in the semi finals. Gage would waltz to the championship game and defeat second-year player Kip Unruh to become the first back-to-back champion in league history.

2023

Mad Dog Bucs the League

🏆 Champion: Justin Madsen

2023 was another season of importance in the league. The popularity of the league was rising, and talks of expansion begun.
After heavy deliberation, the league voted to expand to 12 teams. Hayden Jenkins and Matt Bush were offered two expansion franchises, and the league officially entered a new chapter.
Justin Madsen was a force to be reckoned with all season, finishing with the regular season with a record of 11-3. The playoffs were no different for Madsen, as he ended Gage's three-peat bid with a 134.26 - 117.28 victory in the championship game.

2024

Taco Bowl

🏆 Champion: Lake Johnson

As the league entered its 8th season, competition was at an all time high. A tightly contested regular season saw two teams finish with double didgit wins, as Jim went 11-3 and Austin went 10-4. In a shocking turn of events, BOTH top seeds lost in the semi-finals following their byes. This set up an electric championship matchup between Lake Johnson and Hayden Jenkins. Lake would ultimately claim the title, defeating Hayden 164.96 - 108.78, finally ending his championship drought.

2025

Taco Twosday

🏆 Champion: Lake Johnson

The ninth installment of the GBD Fantasy Football League was as exciting as ever. The regular season was once again tightly contested, with all teams competing for a coveted playoff spot. Hayden's 10-4 regular season gave him the top seed heading into the playoffs, as he seemed determine to avenge his championship loss from the previous season. He would ultimately fall to the same fate however, as Lake Johnson once again proved to be a playoff beast, winning his wild card matchup and defeating Hayden in the semi's to set up his chance at the league's second ever repeat champion. It would be no easy feat, as he would have to face off against Jim Joyner, looking for his second title since 2020. In a low scoring affair, Lake would come out victorious, defeating Jim 109.72 - 71.28 to become the league's second repeat champion.

2026

The Next Chapter

🏆 Champion: TBD

This season promises to be an exciting one!
7 of the league's 10 founding members have claimed a championship, while Trevor, Wes, and Ryker are still searching for that elusive first championship. Will they finally break through, or will we see the first ever championship from an expansion team? Hayden, Bush, Kip, and Chisam will certainly have something to say about it!
This new league season ushers in additional changes, as there will be no more Kickers, a second flex position has been added, and each week, teams will compete in their head to head matchup as well as an additiona matchup vs the league average score of the week.
Who will take home the 2026 Title? Only time will tell...
